# Five-minute demo (use the sample firm)

1. **(0:00) Overview.** "Sample security firm, 24 guards, five clients. Top row is today: who is on site, what was missed, who was late, who checked in away from the site. Below: open incidents, this month's payroll, money owed."
2. **(0:45) The board.** Attendance, worst first. Open a missed shift. Show a correction: "A supervisor can fix a missed tap, but they must say why, and the original event stays on record." Point at the `*` on a corrected time.
3. **(1:30) The roster.** Week grid. Try to give one guard a second shift at the same time on another post: refused. "The database refuses it, even if two supervisors click at the same moment." Open Swaps: someone else must approve.
4. **(2:15) Patrols.** One site requires two rounds a shift. Show the shift that fell short and the missed checkpoint. Open the QR plates page: "print these, one per checkpoint."
5. **(2:45) Payroll.** Open last month (closed). "Two guards are below the minimum you configured; here is by how much." Open the deduction tables page and read the red banner: "Askari states no rate as fact. You enter them, check them, and confirm with your name." Open a payslip.
6. **(3:30) Closed means closed.** Try to run the closed month: refused. "A mistake after closing is an adjustment in an open month."
7. **(4:00) An invoice.** Open an invoice: lines, then the evidence list. "Every shift on this invoice has a check-in and a check-out. When a client says you billed for a night nobody worked, this is your answer." Issue a credit note.
8. **(4:30) The client link.** Switch on a client's private link and open it: shifts verified, late, missed, by day. "No guard names, no pay, no rates."
9. **(4:50) Close.** Price, 14-day trial, next step: add your real guards and one site on a trial firm.

Do **not** claim: that PSRA numbers are verified; that Askari reports to any authority; that the figures are legal advice; that a supervisor cannot fake a check-in; live M-Pesa on their paybill.
