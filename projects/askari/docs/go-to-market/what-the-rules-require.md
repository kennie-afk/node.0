Sources are the research in `~/Software_dev/research/niche-2026-10-03/D-transport-informal.md`. Several were read through search results rather than the page itself. **Verify each before quoting it to a customer. None of this is legal advice.**

|---|---|

What Askari does about each: it keeps the attendance, pay and invoice records those rules are about, compares pay with the minimum the firm sets, and exports everything. It does **not** file, report or remit anything to anyone, and it does not tell a firm what the law requires.
