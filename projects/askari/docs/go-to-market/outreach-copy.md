# Outreach copy (outreach and the first conversations are Kennedy's)

Rules: no invented customers, no invented statistics, no claim of regulator approval, no promise about what the law requires. **The Swahili copy needs a native speaker's review before use.**

## English: WhatsApp or SMS to a security firm owner or operations manager
> Hello [name], I build software for private security firms in Kenya. It records when each guard checks in and out (by the server's clock, compared with the site's location), builds the roster so no guard is double-booked, and checks each month's payroll against the minimum wage you set, listing anyone below it. Clients are invoiced only for shifts you can prove. KES 200 per guard a month, 14-day free trial. Can I show you in 10 minutes? - Kennedy

## English: a more careful version for a firm that is worried about the wage rule
> Hello [name], I am not a lawyer and this is not legal advice. But if you want to know this month which of your guards are below the minimum you pay by, and have your attendance records ready for a client's audit, I have a tool that does that and lets you correct it before anyone else asks. It does not report anything to the authorities. 14-day free trial. Can I show you?

## English: to a facilities manager or procurement officer who buys guarding
> Your security firm can give you a private link that shows, for each of your sites and each day, how many shifts were verified present, late or missed. Ask them whether they can. If they use Askari, they can switch it on in a minute.
