# Five-minute demo (use the sample organisation)

1. **(0:00) Overview.** "This is a sample SACCO with 24 members and a loan book. Top: loans being repaid, how much is overdue. Below: work waiting for you, a loan to approve, a withdrawal to approve, three M-Pesa payments nobody could match."
2. **(0:45) A member.** Open a member: savings statement, loans, balances. "Savings here always equal the ledger; there is no second place for them to drift."
3. **(1:30) A loan from application to approval.** Apply for a loan: show the repayment schedule before saving. Appraise, then try to approve as the same person: it is refused. Sign in as the manager and approve. "Applicant, appraiser and approver are three different people."
4. **(2:30) A repayment.** Post a repayment bigger than the instalment: show penalty, interest, principal split and the next instalment cleared.
5. **(3:15) M-Pesa.** Unmatched payments: assign one to a member, ignore one with a reason. "A paybill payment with the wrong account number is not lost, and not guessed."
6. **(3:45) Arrears.** Buckets and PAR. "This list is who to call this week."
7. **(4:15) The books.** Trial balance and balance sheet: balanced. Download the journal as a spreadsheet. Generate a periodic return and point at the "not official" line. "We say this plainly: it is a summary of your own books, not SASRA's format."
8. **(4:45) Close.** Price, 14-day trial, and the next step: import your member book from Excel on a trial organisation.

Do **not** claim: filing with any regulator; live M-Pesa on their paybill; that statements are audited; that statement analysis verifies income.
