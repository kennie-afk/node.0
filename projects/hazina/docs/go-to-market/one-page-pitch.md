# Hazina: your members, loans and M-Pesa, in books you can hand to an auditor

**For:** a registered SACCO (small to mid-size, not deposit-taking under SASRA) or a non-bank lender in Kenya. **Price (provisional):** SACCO KES 3,500 a month up to 500 members, KES 6,000 up to 3,000; lender KES 10,000 a month up to 2,000 borrowers. 14-day free trial, no setup fee, no share of your loans.

## The problem it answers
- Members, shares and loans live in Excel or a notebook, and the year-end books are a scramble.
- Paybill money arrives with the wrong account number and sits unexplained.
- Arrears are discovered late, because nobody can say today how much of the book is overdue and for how long.
- Rules are tightening on SACCOs and on non-bank lenders, and you want records ready rather than rebuilt in a hurry.

## What you get
1. **One ledger.** Every deposit, loan, repayment and fee posts a balanced double-entry journal. Trial balance, income statement and balance sheet any day. Mistakes are reversed, never edited.
2. **Loans with proper control.** Flat or reducing balance schedules. The person who applies, the person who appraises and the person who approves must be different. Repayments clear penalty, then interest, then principal, oldest instalment first.
3. **M-Pesa that reconciles.** Paybill confirmations are matched to the member or loan by account number. What cannot be matched waits for a person to assign it; nothing disappears, nothing is guessed.
4. **Arrears you can see.** Buckets from 1-30 days to 180+, portfolio at risk, the list of who to call first.
5. **Periodic returns.** Generate a summary of the books for any period and download it. (See the limits: these are generic, not the regulator's own format.)
6. **Applications checked for a human.** Upload a borrower's M-Pesa statement: inflows, outflows, regularity and anything that does not add up. A prompt for the loan officer, never a decision.

## Straight about the limits
- It does **not file anything with SASRA, the Commissioner for Co-operatives or the Central Bank.** The returns it makes are generic and marked "not official". We have not been given the real formats.
- Interest is accrued per instalment and a loan loss provision can be posted from percentages you set (placeholders until your accountant sets them; not regulatory guidance). Your accountant must agree before you treat the statements as audited.
- It is not core banking: no cheques, cards or ATM. No member app yet. No SMS reminders yet.
- Live M-Pesa on your paybill is set up with you and is not yet proven with a real customer.
