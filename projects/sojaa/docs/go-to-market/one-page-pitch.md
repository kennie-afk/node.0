# Sojaa: know who was on post, and pay and bill from what you can prove

**For:** a licensed private security firm in Kenya with roughly 50 to 300 guards. **Price (provisional):** KES 200 per active guard per month, minimum KES 3,000, after a 14-day free trial. No setup fee, no share of what you bill.

## The problem it answers
- You are not on site, and your guards are unsupervised. A client says "your guard was not there on Tuesday night", and you have nothing but your word.
- The minimum-wage rule is being enforced and named in the press. You want to know, this month, which of your guards are below it, and have the evidence ready for an inspector or a client's audit.
- Payroll, rosters and invoices live in different spreadsheets that never agree.
- A guard is rostered twice, or nobody is rostered, and you find out from the client.

## What you get
1. **Attendance you can show.** Every check-in and check-out is stamped by the server, never the phone's clock, and compared with the site's map position. A check-in far from the site is flagged. A shift nobody checked in for shows on the board while there is still time to phone someone.
2. **QR patrols.** A plate at each checkpoint; the round is complete only when every checkpoint was scanned. You see the rounds a shift fell short of.
3. **A roster that cannot double-book.** The database refuses to put one guard on two overlapping shifts. Swaps need a second person. Weekly-hour and rest limits are whatever you decide.
4. **Payroll checked against the minimum.** Each month, every guard's pay is compared with the minimum **you** configure, and the guards below it are listed, with the shortfall. Overtime and holiday pay come from verified attendance. A closed month never changes; a correction is a separate adjustment.
5. **Invoices from verified shifts.** Clients are billed only for shifts where a guard was recorded in and out, each shift once, with the evidence attached. When an invoice is disputed you answer with times.
6. **Your money view.** Who owes you and for how long, and what each client earns you after what their guards cost.

## Straight about the limits
- Sojaa does **not verify** a guard's PSRA, NSSF, SHA or KRA number: it keeps what you type. It does **not report to PSRA** or any authority and does not remit anything.
- It is **not legal advice**. The minimum wage, overtime multipliers and every deduction rate are figures you set and confirm. Sojaa states none as fact.
- A supervisor can still tap check-in for a guard who is not there. The map position and the QR patrols make that easier to see; they cannot make it impossible. There is no selfie or biometric check.
- No offline mode and no native app yet: check-in needs the network and a browser. No SMS to guards yet.
- Live M-Pesa on Sojaa's own subscription is set up with you and is not yet proven with a real customer.
