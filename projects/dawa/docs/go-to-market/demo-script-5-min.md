# Five-minute demo (sample branch, no real data needed)

Have the console open on a phone or laptop, signed in with the sample branch selected (Get started > Load the sample branch, then switch to it). Say each line; do each action.

**0:00 - The problem (30s).** "How do you find out a medicine has expired? Usually when someone complains. Watch this." Open **Overview**: "One batch expired 20 days ago, two expire within 90 days, one product is low. It tells you every morning, in money: this much at cost is about to expire - return it to the supplier while you can."

**0:30 - Selling (60s).** Open **Sell**. Search "Paracetamol", tap it, **Complete sale**. "It took the stock from the batch that expires first. And it will not sell the expired batch even if the shelf still has it." Open **Stock** to show the expired batch with its **Write off** prompt: "You remove it with a reason, and that is on record."

**1:30 - Scanning (60s).** If you have a pack with a 2D code, scan it at the till; otherwise type `(01)00614141999996(17)301231(10)LOT42(21)SN0001` into the scan box and press Enter. "Batch, expiry and serial came from the code. Scan the same pack again" (do it) "and it stops: this exact pack has already been sold. A pack that was never received here is stopped too." (Only works for a product whose barcode you added; otherwise show the message for an unknown product.)

**2:30 - Prescriptions (45s).** Search "Amoxicillin", add it. "It is a prescription item, so the till asks for the patient and the prescriber before it will sell." Fill them, complete. Open **Dispensed**: "That record cannot be edited. Download it as a spreadsheet for an inspector." Mention controlled drugs: "Controlled drugs also need a second person to type their PIN, and the register keeps a running balance."

**3:15 - The cash (45s).** Open **Sales**: "This one is waiting for M-Pesa. Give the customer the sale number as the account reference and it matches by itself; or type the code from their phone." Open **Close day**: "Each cashier counts their cash; it compares with what the system expects. Short or over, it is on record. After you close, nothing can be added to that day."

**4:00 - What it is not (45s).** "It is not connected to the government's track-and-trace platforms, because they have not published how software connects. It records what a report would contain, and exports it, so you are ready. You decide how each medicine is classified. It needs internet at the till."

**4:45 - The offer (15s).** "Fourteen days free. Sign up on your phone now, practise on the sample branch, and add your real stock when you are ready. KES 4,500 a month after that for one branch."

Ask at the end: "What would stop you using this next week?" and write down the answer.
