# Outreach copy (WhatsApp / SMS)

Use your own name and number. Keep the claims to what the product does; do not mention penalties, approvals or government connections.
**Have a native Swahili speaker read the Swahili before you send it.**

## English, first message (short)
> Hello, this is [name]. I built a pharmacy till called Dawa: it tracks every batch and expiry, stops expired stock being sold, and keeps the prescription and controlled-drug records. It is free for 14 days. Can I show you in 5 minutes at the shop?

## English, a pharmacist you have met
> Hi [name], thank you for your time today. Here is the link to try Dawa on your phone: [link]. Start with "Get started" and load the sample branch to practise. When you want to use your own stock I will come and set it up with you. What day is quiet at the shop?

## English, follow-up after a demo
> Hi [name], did you get to try the sample branch? One thing people notice is the expiry list on the first screen. If you add one real delivery with its batch numbers I can show you what would have expired in the next 90 days.

## Kiswahili, ujumbe wa kwanza
> Habari, naitwa [jina]. Nimetengeneza mfumo wa duka la dawa unaoitwa Dawa: unafuatilia kila "batch" na tarehe ya kuisha muda, unazuia dawa zilizoisha muda kuuzwa, na unaweka rekodi za dawa za daktari na dawa zinazodhibitiwa. Ni bure kwa siku 14. Naweza kukuonyesha kwa dakika 5 dukani?

## Kiswahili, baada ya kukutana
> Habari [jina], asante kwa muda wako leo. Hii ni link ya kujaribu Dawa kwenye simu yako: [link]. Anza na "Get started" kisha pakia tawi la mfano ili ujifunze. Ukiwa tayari kutumia dawa zako halisi nitakuja kukusaidia kuanza. Ni siku gani dukani kuna shughuli chache?

## Kiswahili, ukumbusho
> Habari [jina], ulipata nafasi ya kujaribu tawi la mfano? Kitu kimoja kinachowavutia watu ni orodha ya dawa zinazokaribia kuisha muda kwenye skrini ya kwanza. Ukiingiza mzigo mmoja halisi na namba za batch, nitakuonyesha ni dawa zipi zingeisha muda ndani ya siku 90.

## Do not
- Do not say "approved by PPB", "connected to NTTS", "makes you compliant", or quote any penalty.
- Do not invent customers or testimonials. If a pharmacist agrees to be quoted, get it in writing first.
