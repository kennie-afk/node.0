# Pilot agreement: an outline for a lawyer to turn into a contract

This is a list of points to cover, not a contract. Have a Kenyan advocate draft and review the real document, particularly the data-protection clauses, because dispensing records are health information.

1. **Parties and branches covered.**
2. **What is provided:** access to Dawa for the named branches; the features as described on the one-page pitch; support through [channel] during [hours].
3. **What is not provided:** connection to any government platform; any promise of regulatory compliance or acceptance of an export by any authority; offline operation; receipt printing; branch-to-branch transfers.
4. **Term:** trial of [14] days, then month-to-month; either side may end it on [14] days' notice.
5. **Fee:** KES [4,500] first branch, KES [3,500] each additional, per month (provisional; any change notified [30] days ahead and never applied to a period already invoiced). Payment by M-Pesa to the stated paybill and account number; late payment makes the account read-only, never deletes data.
6. **Data ownership and access:** the pharmacy owns its records. Dawa's operator may access them only to provide support and with the pharmacy's knowledge. List who at Dawa can reach the database.
7. **Data protection:** the lawyer to settle the controller/processor roles, the lawful basis, retention, breach notification and the pharmacy's duties to its patients. Record what has and has not been done on Dawa's side (no independent security review or backup drill yet).
8. **Export and exit:** on request and on termination the pharmacy can download its sales, stock-by-batch, controlled-drug register, dispensing log and trace log as spreadsheets (the console has links; no per-patient export exists yet); data is kept for [period] after termination, then deleted on request.
9. **Professional responsibility:** the pharmacist remains responsible for every dispensing decision and for classifying each product; Dawa only records.
10. **Availability and liability:** no uptime guarantee during the pilot; liability limited to fees paid; the pharmacy keeps its own paper fallback during the pilot.
11. **Feedback:** the pharmacy agrees to a short call each week of the pilot; Dawa may not quote or name the pharmacy without written permission.
