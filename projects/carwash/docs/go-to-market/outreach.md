# Outreach copy

These are drafts for you to send yourself. Forecourt has no customers yet: the messages make no claim
about results elsewhere. Prices are provisional. **Have a native Swahili speaker read the Swahili
before you send it**; it is written to be simple, not polished.

## WhatsApp, first message (English)

> Hi [Name], I'm Kennedy. I built a tool for car wash owners that shows, every day, how much money the
> wash should have made against what actually came in (M-Pesa and cash), and where the gap is. Your
> attendants record each car on their phone; Forecourt does the checking. It is free for 14 days, no card.
> Can I show you in 5 minutes? What time suits you?

## WhatsApp, first message (Kiswahili)

> Habari [Jina], mimi ni Kennedy. Nimetengeneza mfumo kwa wamiliki wa car wash unaokuonyesha kila siku
> pesa ambazo kituo kilipaswa kupata dhidi ya zilizoingia kweli (M-Pesa na pesa taslimu), na pengo liko wapi.
> Wafanyakazi wako wanaandika kila gari kwenye simu, Forecourt inafanya ukaguzi. Ni bure kwa siku 14, bila
> kadi. Naweza kukuonyesha kwa dakika 5? Unapatikana saa ngapi?

## SMS (under 160 characters)

> EN: Forecourt shows car wash owners daily what the wash should have made vs what came in. Free 14 days. 5-min demo? Kennedy [number]
>
> SW: Forecourt inakuonyesha kila siku car wash ilipaswa kupata kiasi gani dhidi ya kilichoingia. Bure siku 14. Dakika 5? Kennedy [namba]

## After a demo, same day (English)

> Thanks for your time, [Name]. Here is what we agreed: a 14-day trial at [site], free, no card. I'll
> come [day/time] to set up your attendants and connect your till. Reply here if anything changes.

## After a demo, same day (Kiswahili)

> Asante kwa muda wako, [Jina]. Tulikubaliana: majaribio ya siku 14 kwenye [kituo], bure, bila kadi.
> Nitakuja [siku/saa] kuwasajili wafanyakazi na kuunganisha till yako. Niandikie hapa kama kuna mabadiliko.

## Follow-up if no reply (once, after three days)

> Hi [Name], just checking you saw my message. If it is not a good time, no problem; I'll leave it.

## Rules for yourself

- One follow-up. No third message.
- Never say a number you cannot show ("owners recover 20%" is not something you know).
- If they ask whether it stops theft: it makes gaps visible, and visible gaps are harder to hide.
- Do not message anyone who asked not to be contacted, and do not scrape numbers.
