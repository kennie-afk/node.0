# Forecourt: one page

**Prices are provisional.** Nothing below is a claim about results at other businesses: there are no
customers yet, so there are no testimonials, no statistics and no "recovered" figures here on purpose.

## The problem, in the owner's words

You are not at the wash all day. Money comes in by M-Pesa and cash, attendants record the work, and
at the end of the day you have a number you cannot check.

## What Forecourt does

It compares three records of the same day and tells you where they disagree:

| Record | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| Work | what your attendants record on their phones, one screen: car, services, start, finish, paid |
| Money | M-Pesa confirmations for your till, and cash your attendants declare |
| Water and cars (optional) | a flow meter or plate camera, if you install one |

Each morning your finished day is checked automatically, and the summary is waiting on the Found screen (it is not yet pushed to your phone): cars, jobs, expected, received, the gap, and a short list of
things to look at (for example: jobs finished with no payment, payments with no job, jobs quoted below
list price, an unusually high cash share, a worker who abandons many jobs). **They are leads to check,
not proof that anyone took anything.**

## Why it is trustworthy

- Attendants cannot read the payments, flags or water readings they are checked against.
- A job's history is append-only: corrections are new entries, not edits.
- Your records are never deleted if you stop paying; the account goes read-only and keeps collecting.
- Your data is separated from every other customer's in the database itself, not only in the app.

## What it costs (provisional)

- **14-day free trial.** No card. No setup fee. A flat fee, never a share of your takings.

Sign up in a few minutes, load the sample car wash, and see what Forecourt finds in two weeks of

- A phone number to sign in, and attendants with phones.
- Your till or paybill number, and a short call with us to connect it (payments arriving
  automatically depends on this step; see the first-customer notes).
- Optional: a flow meter or plate camera. We do not supply hardware today.
