# Pilot agreement: outline only

**This is a structure to take to a lawyer, not a contract and not legal advice.** Nothing here has been
reviewed. Prices are provisional.

1. **Parties and site(s).** Business name, owner, the site(s) covered.
2. **What the pilot is.** A 14-day free trial at the named site(s), then month to month at the agreed
   per-site price. No card, no setup fee, no share of takings.
3. **What Forecourt provides.** The console, the attendant screen, daily reconciliation of recorded work
   against M-Pesa and declared cash, and (if devices are installed) water and camera checks. Connecting
   the till is done together and is not guaranteed to complete on day one.
4. **What the owner provides.** Attendants who record every car; the till or paybill number; agreement to
   register the confirmation URL with Safaricom for their shortcode; a named contact.
5. **Hardware.** Not supplied. If the owner installs a flow meter or camera, who pays and who is
   responsible for it.
6. **Price and payment.** The agreed per-site price, invoiced monthly, paid by M-Pesa to the stated
   shortcode or number with the account number. What happens if a payment is late: overdue for 14 days,
   then read-only; nothing deleted; paying reactivates at once.
7. **Data.** What is recorded (jobs, payments, staff names and phones, number plates, optional water and
   camera readings); that it belongs to the owner; who can see it (the owner and managers; attendants cannot
   read payments or flags); retention; export on request; deletion on request at the end.
8. **Staff and privacy.** The owner informs staff what is recorded and why. Data protection obligations
   to be confirmed by counsel.
9. **What flags mean.** A flag is a lead to check, not proof of wrongdoing. The owner is responsible for
   any employment decision; Forecourt makes no accusation.
10. **No guarantee of results.** No promise about the size of any gap or any recovered money.
11. **Availability and support.** Honest statement of hours and response; no uptime guarantee at the
    pilot stage.
12. **Ending.** Either side can stop at any time; the read-only period; export; deletion.
13. **Liability and governing law.** For counsel.
